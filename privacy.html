Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile games published by RIPPLE Game. It explains what information we and our service providers handle when you play our games or contact us about them.

Information we collect

Information you provide

When you email RIPPLE Game for support or to share feedback, we receive your email address, your message, and any details or attachments you choose to include.

Information collected through our games

The information collected depends on the game you play, the services it uses, and your privacy choices.

  • Advertising. In games that use Google AdMob, its SDK can collect your IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics such as app startup time. Google may use your IP address to estimate your general location.
  • Game analytics. In games that use Google Analytics for Firebase, the SDK can collect app events, session information, device and operating system details, and an identifier for your installation of the game. It can also collect advertising identifiers depending on the settings.

How we use information

  • We use what you send us to respond to your messages and investigate reported issues.
  • We use analytics to understand how players use our games and decide what to improve.
  • Google uses advertising data to deliver ads, measure their performance, and help prevent ad fraud. Ad personalization depends on your privacy choices and the settings that apply to your game and region.

Who receives information

In games that use AdMob or Google Analytics for Firebase, the relevant SDK sends the information described above to Google. Our email provider handles support messages so we can receive and reply to them.

  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services
  • Privacy and security in Firebase

Data retention and security

We keep support messages for as long as needed to handle your request or meet applicable legal obligations. Google retains advertising data under its policies. The retention period for analytics data depends on the type of data and the retention settings for the game's Analytics property. See Google Analytics data retention.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK encrypts the information it sends to Google while it is in transit.

Your privacy choices and rights

You can manage your advertising identifier in your device settings. If your game provides in-game privacy controls, you can use them to review, change, or withdraw your advertising consent.

You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you. Include the game name and what you would like us to do. Depending on the law where you live, you may also be able to object to or restrict certain uses of your information or request a copy in a portable format.

Children's privacy

Our games are intended for a general audience. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can review the request and remove the information where appropriate.

Changes to this policy

We post updates on this page and revise the date above when the policy changes.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email RIPPLE Game at ripplegame.work@gmail.com.
